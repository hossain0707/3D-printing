04 / LITTLE QUESTIONS

Need to know?

Will the figure look exactly like my photo?

It preserves selected visible colors and some facial proportions when AI landmarks are available, but uses intentionally cute, stylized geometry. Hidden parts are estimates; exact likeness is not guaranteed.

Can I regenerate or change details?

Yes. Regenerate changes the look while keeping your chosen colors. You can also change hairstyle, pose and shades manually. Re-approve after every change.

Is the file immediately ready to send for printing?

The export includes a GLB preview and textured OBJ/MTL/PNG print candidate. A human operator and the print supplier must validate wall thickness, geometry, color compatibility and manufacturability before accepting it.

Can I place a paid order here today?

Not yet. This is a real interactive design and manufacturing-export release, not an active commerce checkout. Payment, private cloud order storage and operator authentication must be configured before selling.

Where does my photo go?

The default photo analyzer runs in your browser. Your photo is not uploaded to our servers. The optional AI landmark model may be downloaded from a public model host; your photo itself is processed locally.
