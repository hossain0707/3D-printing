03 / GET THE BEST RESULT

Better photos.
Better likeness.

Reconstruction quality depends on image overlap and consistency. Avoid selfies taken days apart with different poses, lighting or clothing.

A

Face & hairstyle

Take front, left, right and 45° views. Show ears, hairline and the full head whenever possible.

B

Walk around the subject

Move the camera around a still person with about 60–80% overlap between adjacent frames.

C

Keep everything consistent

Use the same outfit and evenly lit environment; avoid motion blur and shifting facial expressions.

D

Capture missing detail

Include sides, back and shoes for a full-body miniature. Fine hair and fabric may require manual correction.

04 / BUILT FOR TRUST

Real results.
Clear limitations.

What this service does: reconstructs surfaces and image-derived textures using true multi-view photogrammetry, provided a CUDA-enabled worker and compatible photos are available.

What it cannot promise: perfect likeness, correct unseen geometry, certified physical printing, pricing, or commercial checkout.

Private by design: photos remain in restricted processing storage, projects are protected by private tokens, and customers can request project deletion. All backend retention and security controls must be enabled before opening public upload access.
